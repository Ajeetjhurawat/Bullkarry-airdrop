Everything you need to know to farm safely
Crypto airdrops are token distributions where Web3 protocols reward early testnet users, liquidity providers, and active community members with free governance or utility tokens at Token Generation Event (TGE).

Maintain organic on-chain activity across distinct days, hold a small native gas balance on L1/L2s, avoid transferring funds directly between your own farming wallets, and complete humanity attestations.
